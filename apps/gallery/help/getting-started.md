# Getting started

The Gallery shows every ui-kit component in every state. It is the acceptance
test for the suite's look: if a widget looks wrong here, it looks wrong in
every app.

## How to look around

1. Pick **Core** or **Media** in the title bar to switch pages.
2. Scroll the page. Each panel shows one component and its states.
3. Open **Appearance** from the File menu to change the accent color and the
   glass effects.

## How to get help

Press <kbd>F1</kbd> to open this window on the topic for the area under the
pointer. Open **Help** > **Help topics** in the menu bar to browse every topic.
Type in the search field to filter the list. Press <kbd>Enter</kbd> to open the
first match and <kbd>Esc</kbd> to close the window.

> Tip: The Back and Forward buttons above the topic move through the topics you
> have already opened. <kbd>Alt</kbd>+<kbd>Left</kbd> and
> <kbd>Alt</kbd>+<kbd>Right</kbd> do the same.

See [Core widgets](topic:core-widgets) and [Media widgets](topic:media-widgets).
