# Appearance

The Appearance dialog sets the gallery's accent. Each app in the suite keeps its
own accent, so a change here does not reach the Recorder or the audio editor.

## How to change the look

1. Open **File** > **Appearance...** in the menu bar, or click the palette icon
   in the title bar.
2. Pick an accent preset, or enter a custom color.
3. Click **Reset to default** to go back to the gallery's default accent.
4. Close the dialog. The choice is saved to the gallery's own appearance file.

The **Reduce motion** switch follows the Windows setting. When it is on,
animations become instant and the meters stop moving.

## What the accent colors

The accent paints selection, focus rings, links, the selected segment and the
record arm. The derived accent set keeps the hover and pressed shades in step
with the preset you pick.

> Tip: The status bar at the bottom of the window shows the live accent hex
> and the reduce motion state.
