The **Master** panel on the right sets the output level and shows the mix levels.

## Set the master level

1. Drag the fader up or down.
2. The readout under it shows the level, from -60.0 dB to +6.0 dB.
3. Double-click the fader to reset it to 0 dB.
4. When the fader has focus, press the Up or Down arrow key to step 0.5 dB. Press Esc during a drag to cancel it.

A drag is one undo step, named **Master Volume**.

## Read the meters

1. The stereo meter shows the left and right output levels. The scale beside it is in dB.
2. A red clip light above the bars lights when the output clips.
3. Click the meter to clear the clip lights on the master and every track.
4. The **Peak** readout shows the highest peak since the last reset. It reads **-inf** before any audio. Click it to reset.

Each track header has its own small stereo meter. The armed track's meter shows the input level instead. Click a track meter to clear its clip lights.

The master fader is part of the mix: it changes playback, preview and export.

> Tip: If the clip light comes on, lower the master fader or a track level, then click the meter to clear it.
