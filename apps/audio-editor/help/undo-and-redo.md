Undo reverses the last edit. Redo applies it again.

## Undo an edit

1. Press <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>Z</kbd> or click **Undo** in the timeline toolbar.
2. Or click **Edit** and **Undo**.

## Redo an edit

1. Press <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>Shift</kbd>+<kbd>Z</kbd> or <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>Y</kbd>.
2. Or click **Redo** in the timeline toolbar.
3. Or click **Edit** and **Redo**.

The Edit menu shows the name of the next undo and redo step. The history holds 1,000 steps. It is not saved with the project, so closing the app clears it.

Undo and Redo are unavailable while a take is recording or an export is running.

> Tip: Undo and Redo cover edits to the project. Changing the view, like scrolling or zooming, is not an edit.
