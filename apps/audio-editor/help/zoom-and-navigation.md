Zoom the timeline, scroll it and change the waveform height. These are view changes only; they never change your audio.

## Zoom in and out

1. Hold <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> and scroll over the timeline. Zoom stays centered on the pointer.
2. Or press <kbd>+</kbd> or <kbd>-</kbd>. Zoom keeps the playhead in place when it is on screen, otherwise the view center.
3. Or drag the zoom slider at the right of the timeline toolbar. The readout beside it is the zoom relative to Fit, so Fit reads 100%.

## Fit the view

1. Press <kbd>\</kbd> or click the Fit icon to fit the whole project.
2. Select a range and press <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>\</kbd> to fit the selection.

## Scroll

1. Scroll the mouse wheel to move through the tracks.
2. Hold <kbd>Shift</kbd> and scroll to move along the timeline.
3. Or drag the scrollbar thumb along the bottom of the lanes.

## Waveform height

1. Drag the slider next to the waveform icon in the timeline toolbar.
2. Or hold <kbd>Alt</kbd> and scroll over the lanes.
3. Double-click the slider to reset it to 1x.

The range is 1x to 16x, and it changes only the drawing.

## Follow the playhead

During playback the view pages along to keep the playhead on screen.

> Tip: Press <kbd>\</kbd> to see the whole project again after zooming in.
