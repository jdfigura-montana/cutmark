A clip is a piece of audio on a track. Select, move, trim, split, fade, and change the gain of clips.

## Select a clip

1. Click the clip header to select it.
2. Hold <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> and click to add or remove a clip from the selection.

## Move a clip

1. Drag the clip header to move the clip along the timeline.
2. Drag vertically to move the clip to another track.

## Trim a clip

1. Drag the left edge to change the start.
2. Drag the right edge to change the end.
3. Or select a range and press <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>T</kbd> to trim to the selection.

## Split a clip

1. Place the playhead where you want the split.
2. Press <kbd>S</kbd> or click **Edit** and **Split**.
3. Or right-click the clip and click **Split**.

## Fade a clip

1. Drag the fade handle at the top left or top right corner of the clip body.
2. Or press <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>Shift</kbd>+<kbd>I</kbd> for a fade in or <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>Shift</kbd>+<kbd>O</kbd> for a fade out.

## Change the gain

1. Drag the gain line across the clip.
2. Or double-click the gain value in the clip header to type a value.
3. Or press <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>G</kbd> to open the **Gain** dialog and change the selection.

Right-click a clip to **Split**, **Delete**, **Clip Gain...**, **Reset Fades**, or **Reveal in Bin**.

> Tip: Hold <kbd>Shift</kbd> while dragging the gain line to snap to 0.1 dB.

See [Ripple and Snap](topic:ripple-and-snap) and [Undo and Redo](topic:undo-and-redo).
