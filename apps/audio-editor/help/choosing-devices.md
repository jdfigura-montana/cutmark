Choose the input device and the record settings for your takes.

## Choose the input device

1. Open the **Project** menu and click **Audio Settings...**.
2. Open the **Input device** list and pick a device. The first entry is the Windows default.
3. Pick **Record channels**: **Mono (input 1)**, **Mono (input 2)**, or **Stereo**. A device with one input keeps **Mono (input 1)** and disables the control.
4. Set the **Latency offset** in milliseconds. The range is -100 to 100.
5. Click **OK**.

## The output device

The app plays audio through the Windows default output device. The start screen status bar shows the device name. To change the output device, change the default device in Windows.

> Tip: If your recordings sound late or early, adjust the **Latency offset**.

See [Recording a take](topic:recording-a-take).
