Markers mark positions on the timeline. Use them to jump around your project.

## Add a marker

1. Place the playhead where you want the marker.
2. Press <kbd>M</kbd> or click **Markers** and **Add Marker**.

## Jump to a marker

1. Press <kbd>,</kbd> to jump to the previous marker.
2. Press <kbd>.</kbd> to jump to the next marker.

## Move a marker

1. Drag the marker dot on the ruler to a new position.
2. With Snap on, the marker snaps to the playhead, other markers and clip edges. Hold <kbd>Alt</kbd> to bypass snapping.

## Delete a marker

1. Right-click the marker dot on the ruler.
2. Click **Delete Marker**.

Markers appear as dots on the ruler. When Snap is on, clips snap to markers.

> Tip: Add markers at the start of each section to move around quickly.

See [Ripple and Snap](topic:ripple-and-snap).
