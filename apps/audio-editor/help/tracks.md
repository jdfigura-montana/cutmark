Tracks hold clips. Each track header has a number, a name, **M**, **S** and **R** buttons, a level meter, and **Vol** and **Pan** sliders.

## Add a track

1. Click **+ Add track** under the last track header, or press <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>Shift</kbd>+<kbd>T</kbd>.
2. Or right-click a header and click **Add Track Above** or **Add Track Below**.

A new track is named **Track N**.

## Rename a track

1. Double-click the track name.
2. Type the new name.
3. Press Enter. Press Esc to cancel.

An empty name is ignored.

## Reorder tracks

1. Drag the dots grip at the right end of the name row up or down.
2. A line shows where the track will land. Release to drop it.

## Change the track height

1. Drag the bottom edge of the track header.
2. The height stops at 40 and 400 pixels.

## Mute, solo and arm

1. Click **M** to mute the track.
2. Click **S** to solo it. Hold <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> and click **S** to solo only that track.
3. Click **R** to arm it for recording. The button turns red.

Only one track is armed at a time. Arming another track moves the arm. The armed track's meter shows the input level.

## Volume and pan

1. Drag **Vol** to set the track level, from -60 to +12 dB.
2. Drag **Pan** to move the track between left and right.
3. Double-click either slider to reset it to 0. Press Esc during a drag to cancel it.

## Delete a track

1. Right-click the track header.
2. Click **Delete Track**. Undo brings the track back.

> Tip: Record arm is not saved with the project, so every track starts disarmed when you reopen it.
