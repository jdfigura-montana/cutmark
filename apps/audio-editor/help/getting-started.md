The start screen appears when you launch CutMark Audio Editor. It shows your recent projects and lets you create or open a project.

## Open a project

1. Click **Open project** or press <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>O</kbd>.
2. Pick a **project.json** file.
3. The project opens in the editing window.

You can also drag a project folder or a **project.json** file onto the start screen.

## Create a new project

1. Click **New project** or press <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>N</kbd>.
2. Type a name in **Name**.
3. Choose a **Parent folder** for the project.
4. Pick a **Sample rate** and **Bit depth**.
5. Click **Create**.

## Open a recent project

1. Click a project in the **Recent projects** list.
2. Or press the Up and Down arrow keys to move through the list, then press Enter to open the selected project.

To remove a recent project, hover over it and click **Remove from list**. To remove all missing projects, click **Clear missing**.

> Tip: Press <kbd>F1</kbd> anywhere in the app to open help for the area you are looking at.

See [Projects](topic:projects) for saving and the project folder.
