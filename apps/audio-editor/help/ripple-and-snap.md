Ripple closes gaps when you delete clips or a range. Snap aligns clips to clip edges, markers, and the playhead.

## Turn Ripple on or off

1. Click the **Ripple** chip in the timeline toolbar or press <kbd>R</kbd>.
2. When Ripple is on, deleting a clip or a range closes the gap on its track.
3. The status bar shows **Ripple on** when it is on.

## Turn Snap on or off

1. Click the **Snap** chip in the timeline toolbar or press <kbd>N</kbd>.
2. When Snap is on, dragging a clip snaps its edges to clip edges, markers, and the playhead.
3. The status bar shows **Snap on** when it is on.

## Bypass snapping while dragging

1. Hold <kbd>Alt</kbd> while dragging a clip to turn snapping off for that drag.

> Tip: Snap uses an 8 px threshold. Zoom in to snap more precisely.
