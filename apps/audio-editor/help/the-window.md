The editing window has a menu bar at the top, a media bin on the left, a timeline in the center, a master panel on the right, and a status bar at the bottom.

## Menu bar

The menu bar holds the **Project**, **Edit**, **Markers**, **Process**, **View**, and **Help** menus. Every menu item shows its shortcut when it has one.

## Media bin

The **Media** panel lists the audio files in your project. Drag a file from the bin onto a track to make a clip. The **Import** and **Sort** buttons are at the top of the bin.

## Timeline

The timeline shows your tracks and clips. The ruler runs along the top. The transport deck at the bottom center has **Go to start**, **Play**, **Stop**, **Record**, and **Go to end**. The timeline toolbar has **Undo**, **Redo**, **Ripple**, and **Snap** on the left and the waveform and zoom controls on the right.

## Master panel

The **Master** panel on the right shows the master meters. The master fader sets the output level.

## Status bar

The status bar shows the playhead timecode, the sample rate and bit depth, and the **Ripple** and **Snap** readouts. A finished export shows a **Reveal** link.

> Tip: Press <kbd>F1</kbd> over a panel to open the help topic for that area.
